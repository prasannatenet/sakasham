RELOCATION COST GUIDE

Factors That Affect Packers and Movers Charges

The total cost of packers and movers services depends on several factors, including moving distance, household goods volume, packing materials, manpower requirements, vehicle type, insurance, and additional relocation services.

Cost Factor Description
Moving Distance The longer the relocation distance, the higher the transportation and fuel costs.
Volume of Household Goods The quantity and weight of household goods determine the required packing materials, manpower, and vehicle size.
Packing Materials High-quality packing materials such as bubble wrap, corrugated sheets, cartons, and wooden crates increase the overall moving cost.
Vehicle Type The choice of vehicle, such as a pickup, Tata 407, or container truck, affects transportation charges.
Manpower Required Larger homes and office relocations require more packing and loading staff, increasing labour costs.
Floor Level & Lift Availability Shifting from higher floors without lift access requires additional manpower and time.
Insurance Coverage Transit insurance protects your belongings during transportation and adds a small extra cost.
Storage & Warehousing Temporary storage or warehousing services incur additional charges based on duration and space.
Special Handling Items Fragile items, pianos, antiques, glass furniture, and heavy equipment require extra care and specialized packing.
Additional Services Unpacking, furniture assembly, AC installation, TV mounting, and appliance setup may involve additional charges.
